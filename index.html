STEUERBOT/88

Left Germany? Worked there in the last few years? You may have paid more income tax than you owed. STEUERBOT/88 walks you through gathering your documents and organising your figures so you can file a prior-year Einkommensteuererklärung yourself.

Start the interview → How it works

PROTOTYPE — NOT TAX ADVICE. STEUERBOT/88 is a portfolio demo. It is not affiliated with the Bundeszentralamt für Steuern, ELSTER or any German tax authority. It never submits anything: you review, sign and submit your own return. Every rate and threshold it uses is an unverified configuration value — check them before relying on any number.

> WHO IS THIS FOR

Departed
Employees who worked in Germany, have since moved abroad, and never filed a return for those years.
Paperwork-averse
People who would rather be told exactly which documents to fetch than read the Einkommensteuergesetz.
Self-filers
Anyone happy to type the final figures into ELSTER themselves once they are laid out clearly.

> THE FLOW

  1. Go Get TheseUnterlagen besorgen
    A checklist of documents to find before you begin.
  2. Personal DetailsPersönliche Angaben
    Name, Steuer-ID (checksum-validated), addresses, refund IBAN (checksum-validated).
  3. Tax Years + DocumentsSteuerjahre + Unterlagen
    Choose your years and enter each Lohnsteuerbescheinigung.
  4. Commute DetailsEntfernungspauschale
    Distance and days, priced per km from the per-year config.
  5. Work ExpensesWerbungskosten
    Home office, equipment, phone/internet, courses, applications.
  6. ReviewÜberprüfung
    Per-year estimate against the lump sum; export JSON or a printable summary.

> PRIVACY

There is no server component. Your answers are kept in your own browser's localStorage and nowhere else. The pages load no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers. Exports are generated in the browser and downloaded to your device. The wizard has a button to wipe everything.
